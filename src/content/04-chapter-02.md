title: "On the organizations I work with"
intro: "Four kinds of organizations, across science, philanthropy, politics, and the technology that serves them."
items:
  - title: "Innovators"
    body: "Building novel technology &mdash; working at the frontier, before the path to scale is clear. Typically early-stage startups, research institutes, FROs, academic labs, and private R&amp;D groups."
  - title: "Builders"
    body: "Commercializing and disseminating technology &mdash; moving from <em>does it work</em> to <em>does it scale</em>, and growing the team and product to match. Typically scaling startups and technology companies."
  - title: "Operators"
    body: "Adopting and running technology in service of a mission &mdash; deciding what to bring in, integrating it with existing systems and workflows, and judging whether it earns its keep. Typically campaigns, advocacy organizations, nonprofits, hospital systems, and large pharma."
  - title: "Funders"
    body: "Investing in the future of science and technology &mdash; taking big swings and shaping what gets built. They need scientific and technical diligence before the wire goes out, and ongoing guidance for portfolio companies after. Typically philanthropies, venture capital, angel investors, and family offices."
