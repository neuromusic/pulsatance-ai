Our news cycles and social media are filled with AI hype, but most organizations haven't experienced the revolution and fear they are missing out. The models and tools have matured drastically in the past several months, creating enormous opportunity for organizations that can wield them. The hard part is knowing where to start without wasting time.

I help ambitious organizations figure out how to embrace AI to scale their impact without sacrificing their values.

The work is concrete. It starts with a needs assessment: sitting with your team, understanding what they actually do all day, and finding where these tools fit and where they don't. Then technical recommendations — what to buy, what to build, what to skip. Then training, onboarding, and custom tooling to meet your needs: workshops tailored to your team, software development and agentic workflows built for the nuances of your work. 

Twenty years of work across science, technology, nonprofits, and philanthropy, plus daily hands-on practice with the latest tools — I've been the person adopting them and the person building them. And I bring all of it to your organization's toughest challenges.
