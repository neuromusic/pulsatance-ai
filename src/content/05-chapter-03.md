    body: "Recurring and light-touch. Monthly standing time plus async input. For founders and leaders who need a thinking partner more often than they need a hire."
  - title: "Fractional leadership"
    body: "One to two days per week, three to twelve months. A senior operator embedded in your team, with the authority to make decisions, the scope to see them through, and the context to know when to push back."
