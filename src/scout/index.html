AI-Guided CRISPR Screen for Psoriasis Drug Targets

Zhao, C. & Shih, M. et al. · corresponding Abdalla M. Abdrabou · senior Shana O. Kelley
CZ Biohub Chicago / Northwestern / UChicago · bioRxiv 2026-02-28 · 10.64898/2026.02.26.708368

Thesis Fit
5 / 5
Target discoveryStrong · 🔥 lead of the week

The team's own LLM (VirtualCRISPR) prioritizes novel targets from a genome-wide CRISPR screen, carried through to topical small-molecule leads with in-vivo efficacy — and IP has been filed.

Why this rating
  • AI is genuine and integral. The team's in-house LLM VirtualCRISPR scores every gene in a genome-wide CRISPR screen (>19,000 genes) as a novelty filter, nominating uncharacterized targets (OXTR, ALOX5, CYP3A5) that small molecules then confirm in vivo — AI for discovery, wet-lab for proof. Their own model, central to the platform.
  • Full target → candidate → in-vivo arc. Nominated targets ALOX5 and OXTR were validated; their topical inhibitors (Zileuton, Cligosiban) matched a systemic anti-IL17RA antibody in a psoriasis mouse model, with multi-omics confirmation in 3D organotypic skin.
  • Modality. Small-molecule (drug-repurposing) therapeutics for inflammatory skin disease, from AI-prioritized primary-cell CRISPR functional genomics.
Early-stage company signals

The bioRxiv form declares "no competing interest," but the manuscript's ethics statement says the team "filed intellectual property related to the results." That filed IP is the signal — a concrete commercialization step, with no company named yet.

Filed IP without an incorporated entity fits a pre-incorporation academic team protecting a possible spinout. All academic + nonprofit affiliations: CZ Biohub Chicago, Northwestern, and UChicago (the VirtualCRISPR group).

AI-Designed Oral ACAT1 Inhibitors for Alzheimer's

Agarwal S. et al. · corresponding Sharad Agarwal (sharad@curagen.ai)
CuraGenAI Labs (NJ) · bioRxiv 2026-05-26 · 10.64898/2026.05.21.727002

Thesis Fit
4 / 5
Generative designStrong · company already exists

A generative-chemistry + RL pipeline designs a novel oral, brain-penetrant small-molecule series against a genetically-anchored Alzheimer's target (ACAT1), yielding three nominated leads.

Why this rating
  • AI is the engine. A graph-transformer designs molecules de novo, tuned by three-phase transfer learning and multi-objective RL (docking vs ACAT1, ACAT2-selectivity penalty, ML-ensemble ADMET, synthetic accessibility). ~6M generated → ~7,300 CNS-optimized → 3 leads.
  • Therapeutic intent is clear. A first-in-class oral program against ACAT1/SOAT1, motivated by APOE-ε4 genetics; three leads with predicted BBB probability >0.93 at/above the nevanimibe benchmark.
  • Modality. Small-molecule, CNS/oral — structure-based + generative chemistry, an in-silico lead-optimization campaign.
Early-stage company signals

Competing interests: "S.A., R.P., and P.A. are affiliated with CuraGenAI Labs, which has filed provisional patent applications on compounds described in this work. The CuraGenAI Drug Discovery Platform is proprietary technology of CuraGenAI Labs."

A named company with filed provisional IP on the actual compounds — this one already exists, well past pre-incorporation. Three of five authors sit at CuraGenAI Labs (NJ) behind a company-domain email; collaborators at DRI Biotechnologies and Montefiore.

AetherCell — Generative Virtual-Cell Engine for Drug Discovery

Xie, Z.; Li, W.; Chen, Y.; Peng, Z.; Xiang, L.; Wang, D. · corresponding Zhi Xie
Sun Yat-sen University (Zhongshan Ophthalmic Center) · bioRxiv 2026-03-16 · 10.64898/2026.03.13.710968

Thesis Fit
4 / 5
Target discoveryStrong · all-academic, open-sourced

A generative foundation model is the core engine, and it carries a drug-repurposing program all the way to in-vivo efficacy in two disease models — both pillars clearly present.

Why this rating
  • AI does the scientific work. A Backbone VAE learns a transcriptomic manifold from ~519K RNA-seq profiles and ~2.8M perturbation signatures; MolFormer and ESM-C/STRING-GNN encoders plus a Mixture-of-Experts repurposing head predict mechanism-specific responses. No conventional study underneath.
  • Wet-lab-demonstrated in vivo. Predicted two non-obvious candidates, then validated both: teriflunomide for dry eye disease and dabigatran for ulcerative colitis, each to standard-of-care parity.
  • Modality. Small-molecule drug repurposing via a virtual-cell foundation model — the deliverable is the platform, not a new chemical entity.
Early-stage company signals

Competing interests: "The authors declare that they have no competing interests." No founder role, equity, named startup, or patent — typical of a pre-incorporation academic team.

Single-lab, all-academic (Zhongshan Ophthalmic Center, SYSU). Funding entirely Chinese public grants. Code and weights fully open-sourced on GitHub with training data on Zenodo — an academic, non-IP posture, not stealth.

Small-Molecule Tractability of Cytokines

Raavi et al. · corresponding Arturo J. Vegas
Vegas Lab (Boston University) + Koehler Lab (MIT Koch Institute / Broad) · bioRxiv 2026-04-23 · 10.64898/2026.04.20.719718

Thesis Fit
3 / 5
Target discoveryMedium · AI supporting, not the engine

First-in-class small-molecule cytokine inhibitors (strong program), but the discovery engine is experimental SMM screening — AI (Boltz-2) is genuine but post-hoc, and shapes no candidate selection.

Why this rating
  • AI is supporting, not the engine. Discovery is wet-lab SMM screening (65,000 compounds × 32 cytokines) with rule-based cheminformatics. Boltz-2 runs after discovery to rationalize binding — real structural work, but it selects and filters nothing. Hence Medium.
  • Therapeutic intent is strong. Orally-tractable inhibitors of validated cytokine targets (IL-17, IL-13, IL-23) for RA / asthma / psoriasis / SLE — first-in-class hits at single- to double-digit µM, plus a filed provisional patent.
  • Modality. Small-molecule modulation of cytokine PPIs via a binding-first SMM platform; the differentiated asset is the screening platform + tractability map, not an AI engine.
Early-stage company signals

Declaration of Interests: "A.N.K. is the co-founder of Samori Bio and Epikare and advises Ladder Bio. A.J.V., S.P.Q., A.C. are current employees of Flagship Pioneering, Light Horse Therapeutics and Blueprint Medicines respectively. Authors have filed a provisional patent application through MIT and Boston University on this work."

Strong commercialization + venture signal — the corresponding author is a current Flagship Pioneering employee, and IP is filed but assigned to the universities. Academic core (BU + MIT Koch / Broad); industry ties surface only in the declaration.
